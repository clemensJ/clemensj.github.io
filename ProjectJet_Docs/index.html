Yale Project Jet - The Hunt for Supersonic flight

As Project Lead of Yale Project Jet, I lead the development of a high speed air-breathing UAV, with the lofty goal of breaking the sound barrier. I own the entire system, from airframe to engine. In the club's inaugural year we built a subscale airframe demonstrator and compressor prototypes, and held reviews with industry professionals from RTX. This year, we are sprinting towards complete engine build, static fire, and full scale airframe construction.
